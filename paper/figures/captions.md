# Figure Captions

Each figure ships as `{name}_1col.{pdf,png}` (single-column, ~3.3in
wide) and `{name}_2col.{pdf,png}` (double-column, ~7.0in wide) — PDF is
vector, PNG is 300dpi. Generated by `scripts/experiment_phase/paper/
build_fig{N}_*.py`, all reading from already-git-tracked run
directories (paths given in each caption's data source line, not
repeated in-figure).

**Fig. 1 — System overview.** History (10-step roll/pitch/yaw) passes
through a selector (RecentK or Adaptive-K) that shortens the prefill
sequence, then into speculative block verification: a constant-velocity
draft proposes multiple coordinate steps, one target-model forward
verifies all of them at once against an L2-distance acceptance
threshold, accepted steps advance the carry token and the loop repeats,
and any rejected step triggers a KV-cache rollback before re-drafting
from the last accepted carry token. The loop continues until the full
20-step rollout is covered. Schematic; no data.

**Fig. 2 — Ablation MAE-latency tradeoff.** Full 1,698-sample test set,
same fine-tuned checkpoint. Four configurations: A (baseline, no
selector, no speculative decoding), B (RecentK-2 selector only), C
(speculative decoding only, threshold=0.35, γ=8), D (RecentK-2 +
speculative). D is the only configuration achieving both lower MAE and
lower latency than baseline simultaneously. *Data:
`results/speculative/consolidated/final_table.csv`.*

**Fig. 3 — Speculative decoding: forward-count reduction and threshold
sensitivity.** (a) LLM forward evaluations per 20-step prediction,
baseline vs. speculative (threshold=0.35, γ=8): 20 → 4.21. (b) MAE
across a 7x acceptance-threshold sweep (0.35 to 2.5, γ=8 fixed), y-axis
zoomed to [12.7, 13.0] to show the absence of an accuracy cliff in this
range. Full 1,698-sample test set. *Data:
`results/speculative/consolidated/{final_table,consolidated_results}.csv`.*

**Fig. 4 — Additive composition (per-sample MAE CDF).** Cumulative
distribution of per-sample MAE across four configurations: baseline,
+Speculative, +RecentK-2, +both. The baseline and +Speculative curves
are visually indistinguishable, as are +RecentK-2 and +both — evidence
that the selector drives the accuracy shift and speculative decoding
composes additively on top of it rather than interacting. Full
1,698-sample test set. *Data: `results/speculative/20260802T075640Z`,
`20260802T082009Z`, `20260802T101802Z` (per-sample CSVs).*

**Fig. 5 — History motion speed vs. per-sample MAE degradation.**
Per-sample MAE difference (config D minus baseline A) plotted against
each sample's average history motion speed (degrees/step), all 1,698
samples; the top-5% most-degraded samples (n=84) highlighted. Population
-wide Spearman correlation is negative (ρ=−0.400, p=2.8e-66; higher
motion speed more often predicts *improvement*), but the worst-case tail
is drawn from a higher-motion, higher-variance regime — a fan-shaped
spread, not a simple monotonic relationship. *Data: re-derived history
features (CPU only, no model) plus `results/speculative/20260802T075640Z`
/ `20260802T101802Z` per-sample MAE; see
`scripts/experiment_phase/paper/build_fig5_tail.py`.*

**Fig. 6 — Adaptive-K: opposite-direction result.** Mean MAE before
(plain RecentK-2 + speculative) vs. after (Adaptive-K + speculative),
for the overall population (n=1,698) and for the pre-existing top-5%
degraded group (n=84) the intervention targeted. The two groups move in
opposite directions: the degraded group improves (−12.8%) exactly as
designed, but the overall population regresses (+8.5%) because most
samples flagged for widening were not actually part of the degraded
group. *Data:
`results/speculative/consolidated/adaptive_k_results_stats.json`.*

**Fig. 7 — Absence of a separating signal.** AUC (probability a random
false-positive-widened sample's feature value exceeds a random
true-positive-widened sample's) for five history-derived features:
direction-reversal count, velocity standard deviation, velocity
coefficient of variation, average acceleration, and average velocity
(control check). All five sit within 0.055 of the chance value (0.5,
dashed line); none of the associated Mann-Whitney tests reach p<0.15.
The 10-step history does not contain a simple derived-statistic signal
distinguishing the two groups. n=63 true positive, n=382 false
positive. *Data:
`results/speculative/consolidated/adaptive_k_fp_diagnosis_stats.json`.*

**Fig. 8 — Generalization to unseen data (Wu2017).** MAE for the same
four configurations (A/B/C/D), in-distribution (Jin2022, full
1,698-sample test set, the checkpoint's fine-tuning distribution) vs.
unseen (Wu2017, 300 evenly-strided samples, distribution shift). Every
headline property — the selector's accuracy gain (A→B: −15.25% in-dist.
vs. −14.39% unseen), speculative's forward-count reduction and accuracy
preservation, and additive composition — transfers with no meaningful
degradation. *Data:
`docs/experiment_phase/analysis/GENERALIZATION_WU2017.md`,
`results/speculative/20260823T074714Z` + `20260823T075033Z`.*
